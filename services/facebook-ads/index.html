Facebook Ads / Meta Ads

Facebook Ads / Meta Ads from JN Backlink: targeted Facebook and Instagram ads that reach your ideal customers and turn attention into leads and sales.

What Is Included

  • Ad account and Pixel setup — Correct setup of Meta Business Suite, ad account, Pixel and conversion tracking.
  • Audience research and targeting — Finding and reaching the people most likely to buy from you.
  • Ad creative and copywriting — Eye-catching images, video ideas and clear ad copy that encourages action.
  • Campaign setup and management — Lead, traffic, sales and retargeting campaigns, managed and improved every week.
  • Retargeting — Showing ads again to people who visited your website or engaged with your page.
  • A/B testing and optimization — Testing audiences, creatives and offers to lower costs and improve results.
  • Clear monthly reporting — Simple reports on reach, leads, sales and ad spend.

Who It Is For

Facebook and Instagram Ads are ideal for online stores, local businesses, service providers and anyone who wants fast, targeted visibility and leads.
