Google Ads

Google Ads services from JN Backlink: get to the top of Google instantly with well-managed Search, Display and YouTube campaigns focused on return on investment.

What Is Included

  • Google Ads account setup — Correct account, conversion tracking and Google Analytics setup.
  • Keyword research — Finding high-intent keywords that bring buyers, not just clicks.
  • Search campaigns — Text ads shown to people searching on Google.
  • Display and YouTube campaigns — Visual ads that build awareness and bring visitors back.
  • Ad copy and landing page advice — Clear ads and pages designed to turn clicks into enquiries and sales.
  • Budget and bid management — Careful management of your budget to reduce wasted spend.
  • Weekly optimization and monthly reporting — Ongoing improvements, with simple reports on clicks, conversions and cost.

Who It Is For

Google Ads is ideal for businesses that want leads and sales quickly — including local services, online stores, clinics, real estate and B2B companies.
