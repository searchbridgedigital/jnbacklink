Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly, for example when you contact us through the contact form or by email: your name, email address, website address and the details in your message. We also collect limited, anonymised usage data through Google Analytics (GA4), such as which pages are visited and how visitors use the site. Our free SEO tools run entirely in your browser — nothing you type or upload into a tool is sent to a server or stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to reply to your enquiries, provide the services you ask for, send you a plan and a quote, and improve our website and services. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies from Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, but some features may be limited.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic. Google may collect data under its own privacy policy. Our website also links to third-party services such as Telegram; we are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites or services.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at jnbacklink@gmail.com.
